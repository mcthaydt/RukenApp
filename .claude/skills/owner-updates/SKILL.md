# Owner visibility and early steering

The owner stays informed throughout the work. Updates do not require acknowledgement: continue within the approved brief and permissions. Silence never creates a new approval. When a coordinator is active, it consolidates worker reports into one owner-facing update. Otherwise the implementer handles these duties; no extra session is needed.

- **At the start:** state the outcome being built, the important assumptions, the first step and the first point at which the owner will see working output.
- **During the work:** send a concise update at meaningful milestones and at the owner update interval in Settings. Report material changes, risks, setbacks and consequential decisions promptly. Routine debugging stays internal unless it changes the expected outcome, timing or confidence. If waiting on CI or another dependency, say what is waiting, what can proceed and what will unblock it; do not imply progress that has not happened.
- **Each update covers:** what is happening now; what changed since the last update; what remains and comes next; material decisions made; the latest preview and verification status; and anything the owner needs to decide. Omit unchanged details. Say "No action needed" when work can continue. Prefer completed outcomes and remaining work over an unsupported percentage. Label timing estimates and revise them when the evidence changes.
- **Show work early:** share the first working screen or playable core loop before extending it across the product. Show meaningful visual and interaction changes as they become available, using an available live preview, screenshot or short recording. Label layout sketches, incomplete work and known defects clearly. Identify the version shown. Reuse current previews until the output changes. Creating a public preview still requires the appropriate permission.
- **Expose decisions:** disclose material choices that affect user experience, appearance, architecture, dependencies, compatibility, cost, delivery order or accepted tradeoffs. State the choice, why it was made, its consequence and how easily it can be changed. Announce consequential choices within the approved scope before substantial dependent work accumulates, and continue. Choices outside that scope require approval before dependent work proceeds. Routine naming and implementation details need no separate notification.
- **Keep a visible record:** maintain `docs/superpowers/progress.md` with a last-updated time, current work, completed outcomes within the active milestone, remaining work, blockers, preview links and material decisions. Share its accessible link or location at the start and keep it current with each substantive update. Distinguish owner instructions, agent-selected choices and unresolved assumptions. A reported choice is not automatically owner-approved. The record's writer writes each owner instruction into it in the turn it is given, before the work it changes goes on, and checks it was applied before reporting that work done; a standing rule also goes into the owner's own rule-change pull request that turn. Archive completed milestones and their decision records in `history.md`.
- **Accept steering immediately:** check for available owner feedback before starting dependent work or merging. When the owner redirects or says "hold" on an item, acknowledge it, pause the affected work at the next safe boundary, update the brief and inform affected workers. Continue unrelated authorized work. State rework or consequences plainly. Keep choices reversible where practical; an update alone does not guarantee the owner has reviewed it.
- **At completion:** show the finished result, verification evidence, remaining limitations and material decisions made during the work. Do not defer significant surprises until this report. Final acceptance remains one finished-result review; progress updates and interim previews do not introduce approval gates.
- **If live updates are unavailable:** say so before long-running work, provide the timestamped progress record and use the runtime's available communication channel. Do not claim that the owner has received an update that could not be delivered.
