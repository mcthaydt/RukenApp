Ruken maker agreement, version 1
1. Your game stays yours. You let Ruken host your game and its clips, show them in the feed and on your page, and let members play it, for as long as it's on Ruken. You can take your game off Ruken yourself at any time.
2. It's yours to share. You made the game or have the right to put it on Ruken, and nothing in it breaks the law or someone else's rights.
3. We look at every game. A person checks each game before it goes live. We can say no, or take a game down later, and we'll always tell you why. Ruken is for people 13 and up, so no adult games.
4. Be fair to players. Your game doesn't try to find out who a player is, and doesn't collect their personal details without clearly telling them.
5. How you're paid. From each member's fee we first take off tax and Stripe's fees. Half of what's left goes to Ruken and half to makers. For a member's first 12 months, half of the makers' half goes to the maker who brought them to Ruken. The rest is shared by how long that member played each game, counting up to 3 hours per game per day. Free play, staff play and playing your own game don't count.
6. When you're paid. Statements are final on the 10th of each month and paid on the 15th through Stripe, once you've connected your bank. We pay once you're owed at least $25; smaller amounts carry over. Stripe's cost of sending your payout comes out of that payout and is shown on your statement.
7. Changes. If we change this agreement, we'll tell you and ask you to agree again before your next game goes up.
