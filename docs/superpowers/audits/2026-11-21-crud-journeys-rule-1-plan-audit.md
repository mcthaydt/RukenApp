## Red findings

### R1. The "kept on purpose" exception narrows the owner's rule without the owner

- UNIT: spec 2.2 (build text), 2.3 (review text), 2.4 third bullet, 5.
- VERDICT: red.
- REQUIREMENT: CLAUDE.md, How work goes: "anything that changes what the product is for" is the owner's product choice; owner rule 14:29 "Nothing should be permanent … everything is editable".
- REASON: any brief can exempt a thing from update or delete, and on the short path (lanes 1 and 2) the brief is the request plus the pull request's criteria, which the owner does not approve; so the claim in 2.4 that "the owner sees it in the brief" does not hold.
- EVIDENCE: 2.2 "A thing the product keeps on purpose … is named in the brief with the reason"; 2.4 "the owner sees it in the brief"; CLAUDE.md "The owner approves the finished result and product choices, never the steps between"; section 5 says "Open product decisions: None".
- EXPECTED: making something permanent is asked of the owner, as a decision card, and recorded, before a review accepts the exemption.
- FIX: in 2.2 and 2.3, require the exemption to be the owner's recorded choice (a decision card, recorded with its scope), and change the review bullet to "unless the owner has approved keeping that thing"; fix the 2.4 sentence to match. Files: the spec only.
