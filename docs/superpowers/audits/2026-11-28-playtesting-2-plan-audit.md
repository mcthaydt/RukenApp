## Red

### 1. A re-invited player gets no Your turn row
UNIT: Rules, "One row per person and game"; Your turn paragraph. VERDICT: red. REQUIREMENT: the spec's own "when the maker says yes or invites them, one row" in Your turn. REASON: Remove sets `noticeGoneAt` "so the Your turn row leaves the list", and a later invite turns `removed` back to `in` but sets nothing else: `noticeAt` is kept (it "only" sets when never set) and nothing clears `noticeGoneAt`. The re-invited player's row stays hidden for good, so they are never told the maker let them back in. The spec also never says which field the player's own Remove sets, so the implementer can't tell "the maker hid it" from "the player hid it". EVIDENCE: the Rules line "Remove: `in → removed`, and it sets `noticeGoneAt` … A yes or invite sets `noticeAt` … only when the row has never had one, so remove-then-invite lights the dot once", against the Your turn paragraph. No criterion covers remove then invite. EXPECTED: after remove then invite, the player sees the row in Your turn again, and the dot does not light a second time (if that is what "lights the dot once" means). FIX: write in the Rules: the player's Remove sets `noticeGoneAt`; the maker's remove sets it too; an invite on a `removed` row clears `noticeGoneAt` and keeps `noticeAt` and `noticeSeenAt`, so the row comes back at its old time and the dot stays off once seen. Add to criterion 9 (database): remove → invite leaves the row shown in `listOf` and `hasNew` false when it was seen before. Only the spec's Rules and criterion 9 change.

### 2. The playtest row's Stop button has no behaviour
UNIT: Your turn paragraph; plan unit 6. VERDICT: red. REQUIREMENT: drunk-grandma test (nothing needs explaining), and "Every new control … works" (criterion 13); a control with no defined result can't be checked. REASON: every Your turn row today carries two controls, Remove and a → "Stop games from <name>" button under the sender (`src/features/sends/YourTurn.svelte:129`–`140`, posting to `src/routes/your-turn/stop/+server.ts`, which calls `sendsServices().stop` with the row's id). The spec gives the playtest row only Remove, and says nothing about Stop. If the row keeps it, Stop on a playtest row 404s (its id is not a send) or, if wired to the maker, stops the maker's sends while playtest notices go on ("nothing here reads" `SendStop`). The implementer has to guess. EVIDENCE: the spec's Your turn paragraph mentions only Remove; unit 6 adds a row kind to `remove` and `seen` but not `stop`. EXPECTED: one stated behaviour, checked by the journey. FIX: write in the Your turn paragraph: a playtest row has Remove and no Stop (the maker's words "Kai let you playtest" sit where "From Kai" sits). Add to criterion 2: the playtest row shows no Stop button. Only the spec's Your turn paragraph and criterion 2 change; unit 6 already owns `YourTurn.svelte`.

### 3. The 18+ invite has no answer for the maker, and the named check can't ask about the invitee
UNIT: Rules, "Who may change what"; plan unit 2 and unit 5 (the invite sheet). VERDICT: red. REQUIREMENT: cycle 1 red 6's EXPECTED ("the invite … refuse or hide an 18+ game for anyone `adultsOnlyRefusal` refuses, and nothing is kept"), and the privacy of a friend's age. REASON: (a) `adultsOnlyRefusal(ageRating, caller)` (`src/features/accounts/adults.ts:28`) and `isKnownAdultCaller` only judge the caller. In an invite the caller is the maker; the person to judge is the friend. The spec says "the service takes the same `isAdult` the sends take", but the sends' `isAdult` is the reader's own thunk, not someone else's. `isKnownAdult(db, clock, userId)` lives in `src/features/accounts/age.ts:111` and is not exported from `accounts.ts` (line 23 exports only `ageCheck`, `isAdultOn`). (b) The spec says the refused invite "changes nothing" but not what the sheet shows the maker. If it ticks "Invited Mia." the maker is told something false; if it refuses, the maker learns the friend is not known to be 18. The implementer has to choose, and one choice leaks a friend's age. EVIDENCE: the Rules sentence "An ask, invite or link open for a game that `adultsOnlyRefusal` refuses for that person answers as Wishlist does and changes nothing"; `sends.send` (`src/features/sends/service.ts:104`) writes the row and hides it at read time, and tells the sender "sent" even when stopped. EXPECTED: one stated maker-side answer that does not reveal the friend's age, and a named way to read the invitee's age inside the plan's files. FIX: write in the Rules: for an invite, the playtest service reads the invitee's `adultOn` and judges it with `isAdultOn` (already exported from `accounts.ts`), so no new export is needed; for an 18+ game and a friend not known to be 18, no row is made and the sheet ticks the face as for any invite ("Invited Mia."), as a send to someone who stopped you says "sent"; the friend simply doesn't show under Playing. Add to criterion 8: that invite ticks, makes no row, and the friend's Your turn shows nothing. Only the spec's Rules and criterion 8 change. (If the owner would rather the maker be told, that is a product choice for a card, not this audit's call.)
