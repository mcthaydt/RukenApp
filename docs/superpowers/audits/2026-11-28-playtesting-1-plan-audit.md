The design matches the owner's asks: by ask or invite only, a badge only on the feed clip, Ask / You asked / Take back on the game page only, the maker's "Playtesters" page behind a link on Live with the switch, invite, accept, decline and remove, and Your turn staying a list for players. ## Red

### 1. The invite link can't be shown again
UNIT: Plan unit 5 and Rules, "The link". VERDICT: red. REQUIREMENT: the spec's own invite sheet ("the game's name, the invite link, 'Copy invite link'", made "the first time the sheet opens"). REASON: the link is "shown once in the sheet and kept only as a SHA-256 hash". The second time the maker opens the sheet there is no link to show or copy, except by making a new one, which breaks the link they already sent. EVIDENCE: the spec's Rules ("shown once … kept only as a SHA-256 hash") against What a person sees ("One link per game, made the first time the sheet opens; the sheet has a → 'Make a new link'"). EXPECTED: every time the maker opens the sheet, it shows the same working link until they press "Make a new link". FIX: choose one and write it in the spec. (a) Keep the token itself in `PlaytestLink` (only the maker's page reads it; anyone who can read the database can already change every row), with `@unique` on the token. Or (b) keep the hash, plus the token encrypted with an existing server secret. (a) is recommended. Only the spec's Rules and unit 1 change.

### 2. A "no" can be undone by the player
UNIT: Rules, "One row per person and game". VERDICT: red. REQUIREMENT: "Only the game's maker answers"; the owner's ask that the maker can decline (07:30). REASON: Take back deletes a `declined` row, and Ask then makes a fresh `asked` row. A declined player who presses Take back and then Ask is back in the maker's Asking list, as many times as they like, so a "no" never sticks. Take back and Ask on an `asked` row also move the player back to the top of "newest first" each time. EVIDENCE: the spec says "Take back: deletes an `asked` or `declined` row of the caller's own" and "Ask: creates `asked` (or does nothing if a row exists)". EXPECTED: once the maker says no, nothing the player does puts them back in Asking. FIX: Take back on a `declined` row keeps the row and sets a `takenBackAt` (the player sees "Ask to playtest" again). Ask on that row clears `takenBackAt` and stays `declined` (the player sees "You asked…"). Add a database test: declined → take back → ask leaves one `declined` row and nothing in Asking. Only the spec's Rules, unit 1's schema and unit 2's test change.

### 3. The Your turn wiring is missing files, and its Remove has no check
UNIT: Plan unit 6. VERDICT: red. REQUIREMENT: "A new row lights the tab's dot"; "The row's Remove icon takes the row off the list"; CRUD in `build`; L8 (the diff names only plan files). REASON: (a) The tab's dot is worked out in `src/routes/+page.server.ts:22` and `src/routes/followed/+page.server.ts:18` through `sends.hasNew`, not only on Your turn, and unit 6 changes only `src/routes/your-turn/+page.server.ts`. (b) Remove and seen are `src/routes/your-turn/remove/+server.ts` and `src/routes/your-turn/seen/+server.ts`, and they call `sendsServices().remove` with one `id`. No unit owns them, and a playtest row's id has to be told apart from a send's. (c) No criterion removes a playtest row from Your turn or checks that the dot lights and then clears, so the row has C and R but no D in the journeys. EXPECTED: the dot lights on every page that shows it, Remove and seen work on a playtest row, and the journey covers both. FIX: unit 6 adds `src/routes/your-turn/remove/+server.ts` and `src/routes/your-turn/seen/+server.ts`, with a row kind in the form (`send` or `playtest`). Units 4 and 6 own the dot in `/` and `/followed`. Criterion 2 adds: the dot shows on Fresh, the player opens Your turn, the dot clears, the player presses Remove on the row, and it stays gone after a reload.

### 4. Your turn's words break the layer rule or the one-words-file rule
UNIT: Plan unit 6, and Rules, "Words". VERDICT: red. REQUIREMENT: `tests/architecture.test.ts` (a feature imports only features below it) and "All new words in one file, `src/features/playtest/words.ts`". REASON: `playtest` must sit above `sends`, because it uses `canSend` and `friendsOf`. But unit 6 puts "the row's words in `src/features/sends/YourTurn.svelte`", which can't import `playtest/words.ts`. The plan either fails the layer test or puts playtest words outside `words.ts`. EVIDENCE: `FEATURE_ORDER` in `tests/architecture.test.ts:12` against the spec's unit 6 row and the Words rule. EXPECTED: one words file, and the layer test green. FIX: `src/routes/your-turn/+page.server.ts` (a route, free to import `playtest`) builds each playtest row's line from `playtest/words.ts`, and `YourTurn.svelte` takes the line as data, as it takes a send's. Only the spec's unit 6 row changes.

### 5. "No new cookie" contradicts the signed-out Ask
UNIT: Rules, "What is kept", and What a person sees ("Signed out, it goes to sign up first … and comes back with the ask sent"). VERDICT: red. REQUIREMENT: the spec's own "No new cookie", and the safety Wishlist already has. REASON: Wishlist sends the ask on the way back only to a person carrying `rk_wish_pressed` (`src/routes/g/[game]/wishlist/+server.ts:16`), "so a link to the way back … adds nothing". The same promise for Ask needs a cookie like it. Without one, any link to the way back makes a signed-in person ask to playtest a game they never pressed. EXPECTED: an ask is sent only after the person pressed Ask, and `/privacy` lists every cookie. FIX: add `rk_playtest_pressed`, mirroring `rk_wish_pressed` (30 minutes, names the game), list it on `/privacy`, and replace "No new cookie" with that line. Add a journey step: opening the way back without having pressed Ask sends nothing. Only the spec's Rules, unit 4 and unit 7 change.

### 6. An 18+ game can be playtested by someone who may not see it
UNIT: Rules, "Who may change what" and "Friends"; unit 5 (`/p/[token]`); unit 6. VERDICT: red. REQUIREMENT: the adults-only gate (`adultsOnlyRefusal`, used by Wishlist and Play), and the spec's own "An 18+ game shows its badge and Ask only to someone who may see its page". REASON: the spec gates only the badge and Ask. An invite from the sheet, a `/p/<token>` open and the Your turn row all skip the gate. A maker can invite a friend not known to be 18 to an 18+ game, and the row then names and shows that game in the friend's Your turn. Sends hide such games from Your turn (`sends.hasNew(…, isAdult)`), and playtest rows don't. EXPECTED: the ask route, the invite, the link and the Your turn row each refuse or hide an 18+ game for anyone `adultsOnlyRefusal` refuses, and nothing is kept. FIX: write that into the Rules. Unit 2's service takes the same `isAdult` the sends do. Add to criterion 8: an invite and a link open for an 18+ game by someone not known to be 18 change nothing, and a row made before is not shown.
