## Findings

### 1. RED: a trackpad or wheel scroll made while Back slides in moves nothing

- UNIT: `src/lib/page-moves.ts`, `slideWhile`; `tests/browser/back-then-swipe.test.ts`.
- VERDICT: red.
- REQUIREMENT: the review brief (Back, then up or down, on the trackpad too); the owner's 2026-11-28 07:48 UTC order that every flow works on every input device and never gets stuck; the PR's own cause ("Chrome draws pictures of the old and new pages over the page and sends touches to them"), which holds for wheel moves as well.
- REASON: a wheel move during a view transition is hit-tested to the transition's pictures, so it scrolls the root, not `main.feed`; the fix listens only for `touchstart` and `pointerdown`, and a two-finger trackpad scroll or a mouse wheel fires neither.
- EVIDENCE: probe "wheel down/up during Back slide", computer (Desktop Chrome): 0 of 8 rounds moved, the current clip stayed at index 2, `scrollTop` 1440. The same wheel after the slide ended: 8 of 8 moved to index 3, `scrollTop` 2160. On a computer this reads exactly as the owner's double: the first scroll is lost, the next one moves.
- EXPECTED: a wheel or trackpad scroll up or down made while Back slides in moves the feed one clip, as a finger now does.
- FIX: in `slideWhile`, also skip the slide on `wheel` (capture, passive, removed with the others), and add the wheel case to `back-then-swipe.test.ts` (or a sibling journey in the computer piece), shown to fail without the fix. Files: `src/lib/page-moves.ts`, `tests/browser/back-then-swipe.test.ts` (or one new journey file), `docs/superpowers/progress.md`.
