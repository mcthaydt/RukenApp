## What a person sees

- **Sending.** The share sheet (feed and game page) gains one row above the apps: "Send to a friend", with up to 8 round faces of this person's friends (people they follow who follow them back, D2), most recently befriended first, each 48px, with the friend's name under it (see Faces and names). Pressing a face sends the game: the face shows the spinner alone while it sends, then a green check and "Sent" under it; pressing it again does nothing. If it fails: "That didn't work. Try again." under the face (`role="alert"`), and the face can be pressed again. A person with no friends yet sees no row (the sheet is as today). Signed out, the row is not there. Sending the same game to the same person twice is one row; the second press says "Sent" too.
- **Faces and names.** A face is the friend's page photo (`MakerPage.photoKey`, through the existing page-photo address), and with no photo, the first letter of the page's name on the page's theme colour, as the maker credit on the feed does. The name is the page's `name`, on one line, cut with "…" under the 48px face. "From <name>" on `/your-turn` and every other place uses the same name.
- **Where they land.** "Your turn" is the third tab in the top bar's pill, after Fresh and Followed, at `/your-turn` (one more line in `TABS`, `src/features/feed/tabs.ts`). A sideways swipe or RB from Followed reaches it, LB or a swipe the other way goes back, as between the first two. When games have come in that the person has not looked at (a count of their rows with no `seenAt`, read by the `/`, `/followed` and `/your-turn` loads), a small green dot sits at the tab's top right, inside the pill, and the tab's label says "Your turn, new games". The pill still fits down to 320px with the dot (the dot takes no width of its own).
- **The list, `/your-turn`.** A tab screen like Followed's empty screen: the top bar stays (`FeedTop` with `on="your-turn"`), so Fresh and Followed are one press or swipe away. It has no clips under it, so the list is plain solid page colours, not glass (the owner, 2026-11-27 04:38 UTC: glass only over moving content). It fits the view with no page scroll (the list scrolls inside its box, with no scrollbar): one row per game, newest first: the game's still (64px), its name in bold, "From <sender's name>" and when ("Today", "Yesterday", or the date). The whole row opens the game's page. The row's link (still, name, sender and when) and its two buttons are siblings, never a button inside the link. On the row's right, a Remove icon button ("Remove <game> from Your turn"). Opening the list marks every game on it as seen, and the dot goes.
- **Empty.** "Nothing yet. When a friend sends you a game, it shows up here." and a "See every game" button to `/`, as Followed's empty screen has.
- **Remove.** Remove takes the row off at once, with no spinner; a fail puts it back with "That didn't work. Try again.".
- **Stop someone.** Each row has a → button under the sender, in smaller words but still at least 48px tall to press: "Stop games from <name>". It asks once, on a glass card: "Stop games from <name>? They won't be told." with Stop (red) and Keep (quiet). Stop deletes every game that sender sent to this person (the `GameSend` rows go) and nothing they send reaches this person again. The face of a person who stopped you still shows in your sheet and still says "Sent" (they are never told). Under the list, a → link "People you stopped" opens `/your-turn/stopped`: one row per person with an Undo button; Undo lets their games through again (only games sent after the undo show).
- **A friendship that ends.** A game already sent stays on the list when one of the two stops following the other: it was sent while they were friends. Only new sends need the friendship.
- **Ages.** A game shows on the list only when the person may see its page: an 18+ game shows only to a known adult, as on the game's page. A game that is taken off Ruken leaves every list.
- **Signed out.** `/your-turn` keeps the top bar and says "Sign in to see games friends send you." with a Sign in button that comes back to `/your-turn`, as Followed does, so a swipe never throws a person out of the feed.

Every screen passes the drunk-grandma test: one main action per screen (the rows on `/your-turn`, the Stop button on its card), words people say out loud, and Back on every screen.

## Rules

- **One stop per pair.** `SendStop` has `@@unique([stopperId, stoppedId])`; stopping twice is one row (`createMany` with `skipDuplicates`), so one Undo always lets them through.
- **The site believes nothing the form sends.** `POST /your-turn/send` with `game` (a slug) and `to` (a handle). Signed out: 401. A game that is not live, or a handle with no page: 404. Sending to yourself: 400. `canSend(from, to)` false: 403, and nothing is saved. Stopped by the receiver: 200 with `{ sent: true }`, and nothing is saved, so the sender is never told.
- **Who can send to whom** is one function, `canSend(fromUserId, toUserId)` (D2). It is true only when each follows the other (two `MakerFollow` rows, one each way). The faces in the sheet come from the same rule, so a face never shows that the site would refuse.
- **One send per sender, receiver and game.** `@@unique([fromId, toId, gameId])`; sending again is `createMany` with `skipDuplicates`. Removing deletes the row, so the same person may send it again later and it shows as new.
- **A limit on sending.** Through the shared `RateLimiter`, the way reports do. The mechanism is public; the deploy's numbers are not (the owner, 2026-11-22 20:25 UTC: the exact rate-limit and spam numbers go private, "the mechanism can stay public"). `src/features/sends/limits.ts` holds stand-in numbers, used for dev, the journeys and anyone's own copy, and only when the private Worker gives none. The deploy reads the real ones from the private Worker `ruken-private` through `env.PRIVATE`, the way the feed's order is read (`cloudflarePrivateFeed` in `src/lib/server/cloudflare.ts`): `PRIVATE` gains an optional `sendLimits(): Promise<SendLimits>` in `src/private-env.d.ts`, where `SendLimits` has the same shape as `ReportLimits` (`{ rules: { name: string; rule: RateLimitRule }[] }`), read by a `cloudflarePrivateSendLimits()` beside `cloudflarePrivateFeed` and handed to the feature in `src/hooks.server.ts` with one `setSendLimits(cloudflarePrivateSendLimits)` line, as `setPrivateRanker` is. When the call throws, or answers a shape that is not that, the send uses the stand-ins, as the open order stands in for the feed. A unit test checks that the stand-ins are used when `PRIVATE` or `sendLimits` is absent or throws, and that an answer from it is used when present. The numbers appear nowhere in this repo or this brief. The RukenPrivate change (a `sendLimits` answer on `ruken-private`) is a pull request in RukenPrivate; until it is deployed the site runs on the stand-ins. Over the limit: 429, "You've sent a lot today. Try again later."
- **Only the receiver reads and changes their list.** `/your-turn`, Remove and Stop act only on rows whose `toId` is the caller. Remove or Stop on someone else's row, and Undo of a stop that is not the caller's, answers 404 and changes nothing. The sender is never shown whether a game was seen, removed, or stopped.
- **What is kept.** Who sent which game to whom, when (`sentAt`, from the injected clock), and when the receiver first saw it (`seenAt`). Who stopped whom, and when. Deleting either account deletes every row it is in (`deletion.ts`). `/privacy` says so, with its "Last updated" date. No new cookie and no browser-kept value. `/your-turn` and everything under it are named `GATED` in `src/features/consent/routes.ts`, like `/me` (opening the list keeps `seenAt`): before the cookie yes they show the cookie notice.
- **Pages that keep up.** `/about` (`sections.ts`) gets one line: send a game to a friend, someone you follow who follows you back. `/privacy` as above.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A signed-in maker whose friend (another maker; they follow each other) has a page opens Share on a clip, presses the friend's face: it shows "Sent", and the database holds one `GameSend` row (create). (journey)
2. The friend signs in: the Your turn tab shows the dot and its label says "new games"; a swipe or RB from Followed reaches `/your-turn`, which shows the game with "From <sender's name>"; the row opens the game's page; coming back, the dot is gone and the row has `seenAt` (read, update). (journey)
3. Remove takes the row off, the database row is gone, and a reload shows the empty words (delete). (journey)
4. Stop: the card asks; Stop takes the sender's games off the list and their `GameSend` rows to this person are gone; a new send from them saves nothing and the sender still sees "Sent"; `/your-turn/stopped` lists them; Undo lets a new send through (create, read, delete of a stop). (journey)
5. A friend with no page photo shows as the first letter of their page name, and the face is pressed and sends like any other. A game stays on the list after the sender unfollows. A person with no friends yet sees no "Send to a friend" row; a signed-out person sees no row; `/your-turn` signed out keeps the top bar and its Sign in comes back to `/your-turn`. (journey)
6. `POST /your-turn/send` answers 401 signed out, 404 for a game that is not live or a handle with no page, 400 to yourself, 403 when `canSend` is false, 429 over the limit, and saves nothing in each. (route or database tests)
7. Remove or Stop on another person's row, and Undo of another person's stop, answers 404 and changes nothing; `/your-turn` never lists a row whose `toId` is someone else. (database and route tests)
8. Two sends at once of one game to one person make one row. (database test)
9. An 18+ game sent to a person whose age is unknown does not show on their list; it shows for a known adult. A taken-off game shows on no list. (database or service test)
10. Deleting an account deletes the sends and stops it is in, both ways. (database test)
11. `canSend` is true only when each person follows the other, and false when only one follows or neither does; the sheet's faces are exactly the people `canSend` allows, at most 8, newest friendship first (the later of the two follows). (database test)
12. Everything works with the keyboard alone, with visible focus: Tab reaches the faces in the sheet, the Your turn tab, each row, its Remove and its Stop button; Enter sends, opens and removes; Escape closes the Stop card. (journey)
13. Everything in criterion 12 also works with a gamepad alone (the owner, 2026-11-27 03:08 UTC: "everything should be fully navigable using a gamepad"): the d-pad or left stick moves focus between the faces, the Your turn tab, each row, its Remove and its Stop button, the Stop card's two buttons and the Undo buttons, with focus always visible; A presses; B closes the share sheet or the Stop card, and on `/your-turn/stopped` goes back; LB and RB step between the tabs (already on `main`, #419). The journey drives it through a stand-in `navigator.getGamepads`. It uses the app's shared gamepad navigation; `main` has none today (only the play screen reads a gamepad), so this criterion waits for the "Make Ruken fully gamepad-navigable" work to reach `main`; it is not a unit of this plan.
14. Every button, the Stop button included, is at least 48 by 48px to press; the speaker, the Fresh, Followed and Your turn tabs and Search do not overlap and share one centre line at 320 by 568, 375 by 667, on a tablet, a computer and a sideways phone (the top bar's existing alignment check covers three tabs); `/your-turn` and `/your-turn/stopped` fit a 375 by 667 phone, a tablet and a computer with no page scroll, and a sideways phone as the other screens do; nothing is underlined. (journeys, including `fits-the-screen` and `real-buttons`)
15. `/your-turn` is solid (no glass: nothing moves under it); the Stop card over it is a sheet like the others and reads the glass tokens; `tests/lib/glass.test.ts` passes. Every wait shows only the spinner; `tests/loading-states.test.ts` passes. (unit)
16. The migration's SQL holds only `CREATE TABLE`, `CREATE [UNIQUE] INDEX` and `ADD CONSTRAINT` on `GameSend` and `SendStop`. (unit test)
17. The send limits come from the private Worker when it gives them and from the stand-ins otherwise; no deploy number is in the repo. (unit test)
18. Lint, typecheck, unit, database and browser suites pass in CI; the layer test passes.
