## What a person sees

Before launch only. On launch day (`launch.now()` at or after `LAUNCH_MS`) every piece below goes away: no badge, no Ask, no tag, no Playtesters link, and `/p/<link>` sends to the game's page. The rows stay; nothing is deleted.

**A player, on a feed clip (Fresh and Followed):** when the maker's switch is on and this person is not a playtester, a green glass "Playtest" badge sits above the game's name, inside the band. It is a picture, not a control; the clip's band is otherwise unchanged (Wishlist), so a player finds out more by opening the game's page. A playtester sees Play with the "Playtest" tag in place of Wishlist, and no badge.

**A player, on the game's page:**
- When the switch is on and this person is not a playtester: the same badge above the game's name over the clip, and under Wishlist a → link "Ask to playtest".
- Pressing it signed in sends the ask at once, in place (no new page), and the play tile reads "You asked to playtest. We'll tell you in Your turn." with a → link "Take back my ask", which deletes the ask and puts back "Ask to playtest". Signed out, it goes to sign up first (with the cookie notice first, as Wishlist does) and comes back with the ask sent, but only for a person carrying the `rk_playtest_pressed` cookie that the press set, as Wishlist does with `rk_wish_pressed`; a bare link to the way back sends nothing.
- A playtester (the maker said yes, or invited them), here and on the feed clip: no badge and no Wishlist. The main button is Play with a small "Playtest" tag in it (it keeps the class `play`, so the feed's Enter and gamepad A start it), and one line: "Free until it opens on 30 November." (D1). Play starts the game as any Play does.
- Someone the maker said no to: still sees "You asked to playtest…" and "Take back my ask", until launch. They are never told no. If they take it back they see "Ask to playtest" again, and asking again shows "You asked…" but never puts them back in the maker's Asking list: a no sticks. Someone the maker took off: back to plain Wishlist, with no badge and no Ask for that game. Neither is told.
- An 18+ game shows its badge and Ask only to someone who may see its page, as Play does today. The same gate (`adultsOnlyRefusal`) holds for every way in: an ask, an invite from the sheet, opening `/p/<token>`, and the Your turn row. For someone it refuses, the ask, invite or link changes nothing, and a row made before (say, an invite sent before the game was rated 18+) is not shown.

**A player, in Your turn (the list, D2):** when the maker says yes or invites them, one row: the game's still, its name, "Kai let you playtest" or "Kai invited you to playtest" (Kai being the maker's page name), and when. It opens the game's page. The maker's words ("Kai let you playtest") sit where a send's "From Kai" sits. A playtest row has Remove and no "Stop games from…" button. The row's Remove icon takes the row off the list, not the playtest, and it stays gone after a reload. A new row lights the tab's dot on every page that shows it (Fresh, Followed, Your turn), like a sent game, and opening Your turn clears it. When the maker takes the player off, the row leaves the list too (it would open a page with plain Wishlist). If the maker invites them again, the row comes back at its old time, and the dot stays off if they had already seen it.

**A maker, on their game's Live screen:** a → link "Playtesters" above "See it on Ruken", while the game is live and before launch.

**The Playtesters page, `/make/<game>/playtesters`** (the maker's own game only; anyone else gets 404). Plain page colours over the game's backdrop, like Live:
- The title "Playtesters", then "Floor Fifty. Playtesters play free until it opens on 30 November."
- The switch, one full-width button like "Show how many people played it": "Let players ask to playtest" with an On/Off chip, and a note: on, "Players see a Playtest badge on <name> and can ask."; off, "Off: only people you invite can playtest." Off hides the badge and every Ask link; asks already waiting stay here to answer, the asker still sees "You asked…" and "Take back my ask" (it is their own ask), and invites keep working.
- A blue button "Invite playtesters". It opens the share sheet as "Invite playtesters": the game's name, the invite link (the same link every time the sheet opens, until "Make a new link"), "Copy invite link", and the maker's friends' faces (the same friends as Your turn's picker, so at most 8, `FRIENDS_MAX`; a ninth friend is invited by the link, and the pull request names this). Pressing a face invites that friend at once and ticks it ("Invited Mia."); pressing again does nothing more. With no friends: "No friends here yet. Copy the link and send it any way you like."
- "Asking", newest first: a face, "Sam wants to playtest", when, a green "Let Sam play" and a ✕ ("Say no to Sam").
- "Playing": a face, the name, "Since Nov 26" or "Invited Nov 26", and a ✕ ("Stop Sam playtesting"). It asks once, on a card like Stop in Your turn: "Stop Sam playtesting? They won't be told." with Stop (red) and Keep.
- Nothing yet: the switch, Invite, and "Nobody is playtesting yet. Invite a friend, or turn on asking so players can ask."
- "Back to <name>" at the foot.
- On and after launch, `/make/<game>/playtesters` and its actions send to the game's Live screen.
- Every list fits the view; the page scrolls inside its box with no scrollbar, and nothing scrolls under the bar.

**The invite link, `/p/<token>`.** Anyone signed in who opens it becomes a playtester of that game and lands on the game's page, showing Play with the tag. Signed out, it goes to sign in or sign up and comes back. The maker's own link, or a removed person's, does nothing new and lands on the game's page. One link per game, made the first time the sheet opens; the sheet has a → "Make a new link" under Copy, after which the old link lands on the game's page and does nothing. A link someone was removed after using does not let them back in.

## Rules

- **One row per person and game,** `Playtester { id, gameId, userId, state, via, askedAt?, decidedAt?, takenBackAt?, invitedById?, noticeAt?, noticeSeenAt?, noticeGoneAt? }`, `@@unique([gameId, userId])`. `state` is `asked | in | declined | removed`; `via` is `ask | invite | link`. Ask: creates `asked`; on the caller's own `declined` row it clears `takenBackAt` and the row stays `declined`; on any other row it does nothing. Take back: deletes the caller's own `asked` row; on a `declined` row it only sets `takenBackAt` (the page shows "Ask to playtest" again), so a no is never undone by the player. Asking newest first sorts by `askedAt`, which only a new row sets. Yes: `asked → in`. No: `asked → declined`. Invite: creates `in`, or turns `asked`/`declined` into `in`; a `removed` row turns back to `in` only by a maker's invite (the maker changed their mind), never by the link. Remove: `in → removed`, and it sets `noticeGoneAt` so the Your turn row leaves the list. The player's own Remove on the row sets `noticeGoneAt` too (nothing else). A yes or invite sets `noticeAt` (the row's time and the dot) only when the row has never had one; an invite on a `removed` row clears `noticeGoneAt` and keeps `noticeAt` and `noticeSeenAt`, so the row comes back at its old time and the dot stays off once seen. Seen (`noticeSeenAt`) is set for every shown playtest row by the same one post that marks sends seen. Take back then Ask on an `asked` row makes a new row and moves the player to the top of Asking; a no still sticks, so this is left with the deferred ask limit and named in the pull request. Every change is one guarded `updateMany` on the expected state, so two presses or two tabs never do it twice. Times come from the injected clock.
- **Who may change what.** Only the game's maker answers, invites, removes, flips the switch and makes a link; only the asker takes back their own ask. The maker never asks for or plays-as-playtester their own game. A person the maker blocked from sending (a `SendStop` either way) can still ask; nothing here reads it. Anything else answers 404 and changes nothing. An ask or link open for a game that `adultsOnlyRefusal` refuses for the caller answers as Wishlist does and changes nothing. For an invite the person judged is the friend, not the maker: the service reads the friend's `adultOn` and judges it with `isAdultOn` (already exported from `accounts.ts`, so no new export). For an 18+ game and a friend not known to be 18, no row is made and the sheet still ticks the face ("Invited Mia."), as a send to someone who stopped you still says sent, so the friend's age is never shown to the maker; the friend simply never shows under Playing. The Your turn rows and the dot take the reader's own `isAdult`, as the sends do, and leave out 18+ games for someone not known to be 18. Pages carry no internal ids a person can change to reach someone else's row.
- **Friends** are Your turn's friends (`canSend` and the friend list in `src/features/sends/`); an invite to someone who is not a friend is refused (404).
- **Who may play.** `playBasis` gains `playtest`, checked after own game, staff and free game, and before member: so a member's playtest play is a `playtest` play and never counts toward the pool. It holds only for a row in state `in`, on a live game, before launch. The play route reads it with the membership check (`isPlaytester` on the viewer), so `play` does not import the new feature. `money` and the feed's play counts read `member` (and `free_game`) only, so they need no change; a database test proves a `playtest` session is left out of the pool's roll-up.
- **The link** is 128 random bits, base64url, kept as the token itself in `PlaytestLink { gameId @id, token @unique, madeAt }`, so the maker's sheet shows the same link every time it opens. Only the maker's page reads it; it gives nothing but a playtest place on one game, and anyone who can read the database can already change every row, so a hash buys nothing here. A new link replaces the row, and the old token then matches nothing.
- **What is kept, and deletion.** Who asked to playtest which game, the answer and when, who invited whom; the link. Rows are kept until the account (or the maker's account) is deleted, and `/privacy` says so. Deleting an account deletes every `Playtester` row it is in (as player or inviter) and, with the maker's games, their `PlaytestLink` rows, in `deletion.ts`, before the purge's other steps that need them gone. `/privacy` says so, with its "Last updated" date. One new cookie, `rk_playtest_pressed` (30 minutes, names the game, like `rk_wish_pressed`), listed in `src/features/consent/cookies.ts` and on `/privacy`; no browser-kept value. `/about` is not changed (the owner did not ask for it).
- **Cookie consent.** `/p/<token>`, the ask and take-back routes and every `/make/…/playtesters` action keep something, so they are `GATED` in `src/features/consent/routes.ts`; the game page and feed stay as they are.
- **Later feed pages.** Clips past the first page come from `/api/feed`, so the feed's loads pass the viewer's playtest state for all games once (which games take asks, which the viewer plays), as Wishlist passes the whole list, and later clips show the badge and Play correctly.
- **Gamepad and keyboard.** Every new control is a real button or link and reachable by the gamepad layer and Tab, with visible focus only from keyboard or gamepad (`:focus-visible`).
- **No overlap.** The badge, the band's extra line and the Playtesters page pass the standing overlap journey at 320×568, 375×667 and 667×375, and every control is at least 48px.
- **Words.** All new words in one file, `src/features/playtest/words.ts`, so the screens and the journeys read the same ones. `sends` sits below `playtest` and cannot import it, so `src/routes/your-turn/+page.server.ts` builds each playtest row's line from `playtest/words.ts` and `YourTurn.svelte` takes that line as data (a send's line it still builds itself from `SEND_WORDS`). No "beta", no "tester" jargon beyond "playtest" and "playtester".

## Acceptance criteria

1. A maker turns asking on; a signed-in player sees the badge (and no Ask) on the clip in Fresh, opens the game's page, sees the badge and "Ask to playtest", asks, sees "You asked…", takes it back, asks again (create, read, delete of an ask). Signed out, pressing Ask, signing up and coming back shows "You asked…"; opening the way back without having pressed Ask sends nothing. (journey)
2. The maker opens Playtesters from Live, sees the ask, presses "Let Sam play"; the player sees, in Your turn, "<maker> let you playtest", and on the clip and the game's page Play with the Playtest tag and no badge; pressing Play starts the game and the session's basis is `playtest`; Enter and the gamepad A on the playtester's clip start it too. The Your turn dot shows on Fresh, clears once the player opens Your turn, and pressing Remove on the row takes it off and it stays gone after a reload. The playtest row shows no Stop button. (journey + database)
3. The maker presses ✕ on an ask: it leaves Asking; the player still sees "You asked…". The maker presses ✕ on a playtester and then Keep: nothing changes. The maker removes a playtester after the card (Stop): the player sees plain Wishlist with no badge and no Ask, the Your turn row is gone, and `/g/<game>/play` sends a non-member to the price card again (update, delete of a playtester). (journey)
4. Invite: the maker opens the sheet twice and sees the same link both times; presses a friend's face; the friend sees "<maker> invited you to playtest" in Your turn and can play. The maker copies the link; a third, signed-out person opens it, signs in, and lands on the game's page as a playtester; after "Make a new link" the old link does nothing. (journey)
5. The switch off: no badge and no Ask anywhere for that game, a waiting ask still shows on Playtesters and the asker still sees "You asked…", an invite still works. Switch on brings them back. (journey)
6. The empty Playtesters page shows the switch, Invite and the nothing-yet words. (journey)
7. With the test cookie `rk_test_launch` unset (after launch): no badge, no Ask, no tag, no Playtesters link, and `/make/<game>/playtesters` sends to Live; a playtester who is not a member is sent to the price card; `/p/<token>` lands on the game's page and changes nothing. (journey)
8. Someone who is not the maker gets 404 from every Playtesters address and action; a player cannot take back another's ask; an invite to a non-friend is refused; nobody can ask for their own game; for an 18+ game, an ask and a link open by someone not known to be 18 change nothing; the maker's invite of a friend not known to be 18 ticks "Invited <name>." as usual, makes no row, and the friend's Your turn shows nothing; and a row made before is not shown in their Your turn nor lights the dot. Nothing changes in each case. (route + database tests)
9. Two presses of yes, no, remove or invite, sent at once, leave one row in the right state. Declined, then take back, then ask leaves one `declined` row and nothing in Asking. Yes, seen, remove, then invite leaves the row shown in Your turn's list at its old time and the dot off. (database test)
10. A `playtest` play is not in the pool's roll-up nor the feed's play counts. A member who is a playtester gets a `playtest` session before launch and a `member` one after. (database + unit test on `playBasis`)
11. Deleting a player's account removes their rows; deleting a maker's removes their games' rows and links; the purge test passes. (database test)
12. The migration only adds (criterion test reads its SQL); `prisma migrate diff` is empty.
13. Every new control works with keyboard and gamepad; the overlap, glass, real-buttons, fits-the-screen and loading checks pass on every new screen and on the feed and game page with the badge and the extra line, at 320×568, 375×667 and 667×375.
14. `/privacy` says what this keeps, for how long, and lists `rk_playtest_pressed`; the cookie list test and the privacy test pass.
15. Lint, typecheck, unit, database and browser suites pass in CI; the layer test passes with `playtest` placed after `sends` and before `feed`.
