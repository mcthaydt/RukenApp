This page says when you can get money back for a Ruken membership.

## The short version

- You can cancel any time. You keep playing until the end of what you paid for.
- Cancelling does not give money back for the time you already paid for.
- If something went wrong, write to us.

## Cancelling

- Go to your account page and press **"Change card or cancel"**. It opens our payment page, run by Stripe. Cancel there.
- You keep playing until the end of the month or year you already paid for.
- After that, you are not charged again.
- There is no refund for the time left in that month or year.

## Deleting your account

If you are a member and you delete your account, your membership **ends right away**. There is no refund for the time left.

If you only want to stop paying, cancel your membership instead. Your account stays.

## When we give money back

Nobody can get a refund by pressing a button. A person at Ruken gives refunds, through Stripe.

We always give money back when:

- you were charged after your membership had already ended.

## What a refund means for makers

Makers are paid from members' fees. When a member gets money back, the makers' share goes down to match.

- A refund counts in the month it happens. Months before it are not changed.
- A member who gets all their money back no longer counts toward makers' pay, from that month on.

The maker agreement says how makers are paid: [maker agreement](/legal/maker-agreement).
