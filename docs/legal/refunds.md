# Cancelling and refunds

**Draft for review. Not legal advice. A lawyer gives the final say.** The Owner, 2026-11-14: "Okay we need to comply with law, have a very strict and discouraging refund policy, and if possible, none for the games themselves". This page gives refunds only where the law requires one. The reasons and sources are in `research.md`, section 4.

Takes effect: 30 November 2026.

## The short version

- **All payments are final. We don't give refunds**, except the few the law requires, listed below.
- Cancelling stops the next payment. It doesn't give money back.
- You can cancel any time, online. You keep playing until the end of what you paid for.

## Before you pay

Please be sure before you join. When you press **Join and pay**, you agree that:

- your membership starts straight away;
- it renews by itself until you cancel;
- **you won't get money back** for any month or year you've paid for, even if you stop playing, cancel early, delete your account, or a game you liked leaves Ruken.

## Cancelling

- Go to your account page and press **"Change card or cancel"**. It opens our payment page, run by Stripe. Cancel there.
- You keep playing until the end of the month or year you already paid for.
- After that, you are not charged again.
- There is no money back for the time left in that month or year.

## Deleting your account

If you are a member and you delete your account, your membership **ends right away**. There is no money back for the time left.

If you only want to stop paying, cancel your membership instead. Your account stays.

## The only times you get money back

We give money back only when the law says we must:

1. **We charged you by mistake.** For example, we charged you after you had cancelled. Write to us and we'll put it right.
2. **You live in the EU or the UK** [LAWYER TO CONFIRM]. The law gives you 14 days from joining to change your mind. When you join, you ask us to start your membership straight away. If you change your mind within the 14 days, we keep the part for the days you had, and give back only the rest. After 14 days, there is no money back. To change your mind, write to contact@ruken.io within the 14 days.
3. **You were under 18 when you paid** [LAWYER TO CONFIRM]. Where the law lets someone under 18 undo a purchase, write to us.
4. **Any other right the law gives you** that can't be taken away. Nothing on this page takes those away.

We don't give money back for anything else. That includes: not playing, playing less than you planned, forgetting to cancel, a game leaving Ruken, a game not working on your device, or changing your mind after 14 days.

## How to ask

Write to **contact@ruken.io** from the email address on your account. Say which payment, and which of the reasons above applies. If none of them applies, the answer is no.

## Disputes with your bank

If you ask your bank to take a payment back, please write to us first. We may be able to sort it out.

[LAWYER TO CONFIRM] Proposed: while a payment is disputed with your bank, your membership is paused. If the bank gives you the money back, your membership ends. We may refuse a new membership to an account that took a payment back without a reason above.

## What a payment taken back means for makers

Makers are paid from members' fees. When a bank takes a payment back, or Ruken hands one back for a reason above, the makers' share goes down to match.

- It counts in the month it happens. Months before it are not changed.
- A member whose whole payment was taken back no longer counts toward makers' pay, from that month on.

The maker agreement says how makers are paid: [maker agreement](/legal/maker-agreement).
