## What Ruken is

Ruken is a place to find and play web games.

- **Watching is free.** Anyone can watch game clips in the feed. You don't need an account.
- **Playing needs a membership.** Members can play every game on Ruken. Some games may be free for everyone. When a game is free, it says so.
- **Makers put their games on Ruken.** A maker is anyone who adds a game. Each maker has a short page with their games and their links.

## Cancelling

- Go to your account page and press **"Change card or cancel"**. It opens our payment page, run by Stripe. Cancel there.
- When you cancel, you keep playing until the end of the month or year you already paid for. Then it stops, and you are not charged again.
- **All payments are final.** Cancelling does not give money back for the time you already paid for. We give money back only where the law requires it; our [refunds page](./refunds.md) lists those times.

## Deleting your account

- You can delete your account from your account page.
- If you are a member, your membership **ends right away** when you ask. There is no refund for the time left.
- You then have **14 days** to change your mind. Just sign in again. Signing in brings back your account, not your membership.
- After 14 days, we remove your details. Our [privacy page](./privacy.md) says what is removed and what is kept.

## Games on Ruken

- Games are made by makers, not by Ruken. A person at Ruken checks each game before it goes live.
- Ruken is for people 13 and up. We don't accept adult games.
- A game plays in a locked-down window. It never gets your sign-in or your account details.
- A maker can take their game off Ruken at any time. A game you liked may go away.

## How to behave

- Don't try to play games without paying, or help others do that.
- Don't try to break into Ruken, or into anyone's account.
- Don't use Ruken to break the law or to hurt anyone.
- Don't put anything on a maker's page that you don't have the right to share.

Staff can take down a game or a maker's page, or suspend an account, when the rules are broken. We always say why.

**Reporting a game or a page.** If something on Ruken breaks the law or uses your work without permission, write to contact@ruken.io with a link to it and what is wrong. [LAWYER TO CONFIRM]

## Makers

If you put a game on Ruken, you also agree to the maker agreement. It says what you let Ruken do with your game, and how and when you are paid. You agree to it once, when you send your first game.

How makers are paid, in short:

- From each member's fee, tax and Stripe's fees come off first. Half of what is left goes to Ruken, half to makers.
- For a member's first 12 months, half of the makers' half goes to the maker who brought them to Ruken.
- The rest is shared by how long that member played each game, up to 3 hours per game per day.
- Free play, staff play and playing your own game don't count.
- Statements are final on the 10th of each month and paid on the 15th, through Stripe, once you've connected your bank. For now, only bank accounts in the United States can be connected.
- We pay once you're owed at least $25. Smaller amounts carry over. Stripe's cost of sending your payout comes out of that payout.

The maker agreement is what counts. If this list and the agreement differ, the agreement wins.

**Taxes for makers** [LAWYER TO CONFIRM]: when you connect your bank, Stripe asks for your tax details for us (in the United States, the details on a Form W-9). If we pay you enough in a year for the tax rules to need it, we send you a tax form (a Form 1099) through Stripe, and send a copy to the IRS. You are responsible for your own taxes on what we pay you.

**A new maker agreement** [LAWYER TO CONFIRM]: a lawyer is writing a fuller maker agreement. When it is ready, we'll show it to every maker, and you'll need to agree to it before your next payout. Until you do, version 1 stays in place for games already on Ruken.

Read it here: [maker agreement](/legal/maker-agreement).
