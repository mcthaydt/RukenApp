## Talk to us

The quickest way is to **chat with us** on Ruken, or **text us** at [OWNER: text number]. Ruken's assistant answers first. It's an AI, not a person, and it says so. When you need a person, it passes your message to the Ruken team, and a person replies.

You can also email **contact@ruken.io**.

We don't take phone calls. Please chat, text or email.

Use any of these for anything: a question, a problem with a payment, fixing your data, or reporting a game or a page. If you have an account, write from the email or phone number on it, so we can find you.

To get a copy of your data, go to **Settings** and press **"Request my data"**.
