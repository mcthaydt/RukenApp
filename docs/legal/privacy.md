Takes effect: 30 November 2026.

This page says what Ruken keeps about you, why, who helps us keep it, and how long we keep it.

Ruken is run by **Ruken App LLC**, [OWNER: postal address]. Write to us at **contact@ruken.io**.

**We keep nothing about you until you agree.** You can read Ruken's public pages without agreeing. Before you sign up, sign in, join or play, we ask you to agree to what this page lists. If you say "Not now", we keep nothing, not even your answer.

## The short version

- Watching clips needs no account.
- To play, we need your email and a password. We check your age, but we don't keep your date of birth.
- When you sign in, we keep your device's internet address, to keep your account safe.
- Stripe handles your card. We never see your card number.
- We count how long members play each game, so we can pay the makers.
- Ruken has no ads. **We do not sell your data.**
- You can delete your account from your account page. After 14 days, your details are gone.

## What we keep, and why

### Your account

- **Your email address.** To sign you in, and to send you account emails.
- **Your password.** Kept scrambled, so nobody at Ruken can read it.
- **Your age.** At sign-up we ask for your date of birth, to check you are 13 or older. We don't keep the date. We keep only the day you turn 18, so we know if you are under 18. If you are under 13, we keep nothing.
- **Where you are signed in.** For each device you sign in on, we keep its internet address (IP address) and the name of its browser. A sign-in lasts up to 30 days, or until you sign out.
- **Too many tries.** We count requests from each internet address for a short time, to slow down people trying to break in.
- **Failed sign-ins.** If someone types the wrong password for an email address many times, we pause sign-in for that address for an hour, to keep it safe. We keep the address scrambled for this, not in plain text.

### Your membership and payments

- **Stripe takes your payment.** Your card details go to Stripe, not to us.
- **We keep a record of each payment**: what you paid, the tax, and Stripe's fees. We need this to run the membership and to pay makers.
- **We keep whether your membership is running**, and when it renews or ends.

### Which maker brought you

- After you agree, when you open a maker's page or a link to a game, we put **one small file (a cookie)** in your browser. It remembers which maker's link you came through, which link, and when.
- If you then become a member, we record that maker, so they get a bonus for bringing you. That is part of how makers are paid.
- The cookie lasts up to 400 days, or until a newer maker link replaces it. You can clear it in your browser at any time.

### Other cookies

All of these are set only after you agree:

- **A cookie that remembers you agreed**, so we don't ask on every page. It lasts 6 months, then we ask again.
- **A sign-in cookie** keeps you signed in. Without it, you would have to sign in on every page. It lasts up to 30 days, or until you sign out.
- **A short "welcome back" cookie** shows one message after you undo deleting your account. It lasts 5 minutes.

We don't use cookies for ads or for tracking you on other sites. When you pay, you are on Stripe's own page, and Stripe uses its own cookies there under [Stripe's privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).

### What you play

- Each time you press Play, we record which game you played and when.
- While a game is on your screen, your browser tells us every few seconds that you are still there. We use this to measure how long you played.
- **Why:** members' fees are shared with makers by how long members played each of their games.
- The game itself never gets your sign-in or your account details.

### Games you want to know about

If you ask us to tell you when a game is out, we keep that list, and a note that we emailed you.

### If you are a maker

- **Your page**: your short address, photo, one line about you, your links and your colours. Your page is public.
- **How many people visited your page and tapped your links.** We keep only the counts, not who.
- **Your games and their clips.** We copy your game's files from GitHub and keep them, with your clips, so members can play and watch.
- **Your GitHub connection**: which repositories you let Ruken read.
- **That you agreed to the maker agreement**, which version, and when.
- **Your bank details are held by Stripe, not by Ruken.** We keep your statements and what we paid you.

### Logs

Every request to any website carries your device's internet address (IP address). Our hosting company, Cloudflare, needs it to send you the page, before we can even ask you anything. We don't keep it in our logs. Our own log lines say only what went wrong, never your email or internet address, and Cloudflare keeps them for 7 days so we can find and fix problems.

## How long we keep it

- **While you have an account**, we keep what this page lists.
- **When you delete your account**, you are signed out everywhere at once, and the sign-in records for your devices are removed. You have **14 days** to change your mind by signing in again.
- **After 14 days**, we remove: your email address, your age, your maker page and its links, your photo, your GitHub connection, your list of games, and any sign-in links we sent you.
- **What stays after that:** a few records with no name or email on them. These are your payment records, your play records, which maker brought you, and, for makers, that you agreed to the maker agreement. We keep them so our payment records and makers' pay stay correct.
- **Stripe keeps its own records** of your payments, under its own rules.

## Your choices

- **Delete your account:** go to your account page and press **"Delete my account"**.
- **Get a copy of your data**, or ask us to **delete or fix** something: email **contact@ruken.io** from the email address on your account.
- **How we check it's you** [OWNER: question 1 in `research.md`; proposed]: we write back to the email address on the account with a link. We act only after you press it. If you write from a different address, we ask you to write from the one on your account. We never ask for your password.
- **Take back your agreement** at any time with the **Cookies** link at the bottom of every page. That signs you out on that browser and removes our cookies from it. Your account stays until you delete it.

## Children and teenagers

Ruken is for people 13 and up. If you are under 13, you can't make an account, and we keep nothing you typed at sign-up.

If you think a child under 13 has an account, please tell us at contact@ruken.io, and we'll delete it.

**If you are 13 to 17** [LAWYER TO CONFIRM; `research.md` section 2]:
